Signature write-on — the Osmo Draw Path on Scroll resource (data-draw-scroll-*). DrawSVG can only animate a stroke, never a fill, so the drawn path here is the white stroke inside an SVG <mask>; the filled signature underneath is revealed through it. The mask centreline was extracted from the filled artwork by skeletonising the ink, so it runs along the writing direction instead of around its outline. Two subpaths in one d give the pen-lift before the final flourish.

Same component, second instance — three separate lines in one SVG, each marked [data-draw-scroll-path], drawn in sequence via data-draw-scroll-stagger. This is the plain stroke case (no mask), and it uses data-draw-scroll-start / data-draw-scroll-end to trigger on a different window than the signature above — the two instances run independently.

AVPN Leading Voice

AVPN is beginning to speak its own voice in the impact ecosystem, sharing knowledge and viewpoints with the rest of the world.

Our Members

AVPN is Asia’s largest platform for social impact, where over 700 organisations from across 43 markets collaborate to mobilise capital. Grounded in deep regional expertise and partnerships, AVPN is building a thriving ecosystem that accelerates measurable progress toward inclusive growth, empowering Asia to lead the way in global impact.

Corporate/Financial Institution

Mainstream companies that invest directly in social impact through Corporate social responsibility (CSR) or through establishing a corporate foundation.

Family Office

Wealth management advisories or establishment for high net worth and ultra-high net worth individuals.

Foundations/Trust

Non-profit organisations that fund social causes. Rather than running their own activities, trusts and foundations achieve their objectives by giving money to Social Purpose Organisations (SPOs) which use the money to run their activities.

Government-related

Statutory boards, government organisations and associations, and large international organisations that provide governments a platform to cooperate.

Impact Fund

Funds that make investments to social enterprises and businesses with a sustainability focus, with the intention to generate social and environmental impact alongside a financial return.

Incubator/Accelerator

Organisations that provide facilities, expertise and other forms of non-monetary support to nurture young enterprises and entrepreneurs.

Intermediary

Intermediaries that help bridge between investors and investees to help with deal sourcing, management and measurement, and often provide some capacity building for the investee organisations.

International NGO

International Non-Governmental Organisations (NGOs) that provide funding for local NGOs to deepen their impact.

Network

Online and offline organisations that bring diverse stakeholders together.

Private Equity/Venture Capital

Private equity and venture capital firms engage in direct investments with privately held companies ranging from startups to mature companies.

Service Provider

Businesses and nonprofits that provide support services to the social investment ecosystem such as consulting, legal support and talent management.

Universities/Research

Universities, academies, research institutes and think tanks that are engaged in the social economy.

AVPN launched its West Asia Hub in Abu Dhabi, its first regional headquarters outside Singapore and its main hub for the region. 5th International Conference on Early Childhood Development

AVPN joined as Knowledge Partner for the 5th International Conference on Early Childhood Development, hosted in Islamabad by Vision member Rupani Development Initiatives alongside UNICEF, government ministries and multilateral partners. The convening brought together federal and provincial ministers, senior government stakeholders and practitioners around Pakistan's early childhood development agenda, marking significant momentum following the approval of the National ECD Policy Framework. AVPN participated on the ground and contributed a social investment and impact capital lens across the programme.

7–8 April 2026
Pakistan

“Connection, Co-existence and Resilience” Community Resilience Ecosystem Co-creation Seminar

The seminar convened experts and practitioners from philanthropy, corporate CSR, academia, nonprofits and media to explore how multi-stakeholder collaboration can strengthen community resilience amid climate change, technological disruption and social transition. Discussions covered resilience theory and systems thinking, Asia-level capital mobilisation, local community resilience practices, education and relationship-building, and cross-sector collaboration. AVPN contributed a regional capital mobilisation perspective, highlighting the shift from passive philanthropy to transformational partnership and the role of innovative finance, including blended finance, in supporting resilient community ecosystems. The event also marked the launch of the 2026 CapitaLand Community Resilience Grant Programme application channel.

5 June 2026

Breakfast Roundtable: Global Expansion and Collaboration Opportunities for the Korean Impact Ecosystem

AVPN convened an invitation-only high-level roundtable with Korean foundations, development actors and impact ecosystem leaders. The discussion focused on continuum of capital, outcome-based funding, trust / due diligence, and pathways for Korean social innovation and hybrid finance models to connect with Asia.

Upcoming Initiatives

ASEAN CSR Workgroup

The ASEAN CSR Workgroup connects corporate leaders, policymakers, and civil society across Southeast Asia to align business practices with regional sustainability goals. Through strategic peer exchange and co-investment, the initiative drives corporate social responsibility, enhances governance standards, and accelerates cross-border impact across emerging ASEAN markets.

Nexa Lighthouse

Nexa Lighthouse serves as a regional beacon for high-impact innovation, showcasing scalable solutions to Asia’s complex social and environmental challenges. By providing targeted catalytic capital, expert mentorship, and rigorous impact validation, the initiative de-risks early-stage models and guides strategic funding toward transformative, sustainable outcomes.

Youth Mental Health Ecosystems Strengthening

AVPN is strengthening Indonesia's youth mental health ecosystem through cross-sector collaboration, policy support, and national-subnational stakeholder alignment. By bridging implementation gaps across government, philanthropy, civil society, and youth-led organizations, the initiative promotes clearer roles and shared priorities to drive coordinated local and regional action.

Nutrition Knowledge Hub

The Nutrition Knowledge Hub bridges research, policy, and field practice to address regional food security and health challenges. By synthesizing data-driven insights, facilitating multi-sector dialogues, and sharing actionable toolkits, the hub empowers decision-makers and funders to scale evidence-based nutrition solutions across vulnerable Asian communities.

15&Forward

After 15 years of bringing together philanthropists, impact investors, corporates, policy makers, and social sector leaders, we have reached a level of maturity, community reach, trusted relationships, and knowledge capability that allows us to take on a more intentional leadership role in shaping Asia-led solutions and strengthening Asia's impact ecosystem. We are strengthening how we activate our product portfolio, market presence, and practitioner voices, to shape regional and global discourse on mobilising capital for impact in Asia.

New Engagement Pathways

From 1 July 2026, AVPN has transitioned its three-tier membership structure to a new framework centred around two engagement pathways:

Discovery

A flexible community that enables organisations to access the AVPN ecosystem and engage through a combination of complimentary and pay-per-use opportunities.

Vantage

A relationship-led circle designed for organisations seeking deeper strategic engagement, dedicated support, curated connections, and leadership opportunities.

AVPN Products Evolution

Our product portfolio covers four thematic areas: Climate Action, Health Impact, Gender Equality and Economic Inclusion. Across these thematic areas, we apply a set of cross-cutting levers to unlock new solutions and capital flow. Our current levers are - AI, Impact Investing, and Faith-based Giving.
